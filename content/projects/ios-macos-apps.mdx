## Problem

Engineering workflows increasingly happen away from the office — on-site, in the field, in meetings. Web-based tools fall short when connectivity is unreliable, interaction needs to be fast and tactile, or device hardware like the camera, GPS, or sensors needs to be tightly integrated.

## Approach

A portfolio of native iOS and macOS apps built in Swift and SwiftUI, each targeting a specific engineering workflow. On-device Core ML models handle inference without cloud round-trips. ARKit and RealityKit support AR inspection overlays. Apps are designed for professional engineering contexts — not consumer UX patterns adapted for industrial use.

## Result

Engineering tasks that required a laptop, internet connection, or desktop software can now be completed on an iPhone or iPad in the field. Response times are instant, data stays on-device where required, and the interface fits the workflow rather than working around it.
