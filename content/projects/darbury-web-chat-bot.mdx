## Problem

Consultancy websites are passive. Visitors browse, can't find the specific answer they need, and leave without making contact. The information is there — the service descriptions, project examples, relevant expertise — but it isn't surfaced in response to what the visitor is actually asking.

## Approach

The Darbury Web Chat Bot embeds a Claude-powered assistant directly in the website. It is trained on the portfolio, services, and engineering domain knowledge — so it responds to genuine engineering questions, qualifies enquiries, and routes visitors towards relevant work or contact prompts. All AI calls happen server-side; no client credentials are exposed.

## Result

Visitor engagement increases significantly. Enquiries that arrive are pre-qualified — visitors who've had a real conversation about their problem before submitting a contact form convert at higher rates and arrive with clearer briefs.
