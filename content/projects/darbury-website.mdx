## Problem

A consultancy website that lists services is passive. Potential clients arrive, browse, and leave without ever engaging — missing the chance to have the conversation that would determine if there's a fit.

## Approach

The Darbury website (darbury.com) includes a persistent Haiku-powered chatbot that speaks as Dave, understands the portfolio, and can direct visitors to relevant work or prompt them to make contact. Built on Next.js and deployed to Vercel, with Anthropic's API called server-side only.

## Result

The chatbot handles the first-touch conversation that would otherwise be lost. Visitors who engage with it convert to contact form submissions at significantly higher rates than those who don't.
