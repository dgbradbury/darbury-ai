## Problem

DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control) documentation for Six Sigma projects is time-consuming to produce and highly formulaic — exactly the kind of structured work that AI handles well but most teams still do manually.

## Approach

A guided workflow collects the project parameters — process name, problem statement, metrics, team — and passes them through a structured Claude prompt that generates a complete DMAIC document with all five phases pre-populated, formatted for review rather than drafting from scratch.

## Result

DMAIC project documents that previously took a full day to draft are generated in minutes, ready for team review and refinement. The client reported a 75% reduction in documentation time on their first project.
