## Problem

Construction project managers need to communicate complex sequencing plans to site teams who struggle to interpret Gantt charts and schedule spreadsheets alone. Delays caused by sequencing misunderstandings cost time and money on every large-scale build.

## Approach

The 4D Construction Planner links schedule data directly to 3D model elements, animating the build sequence over time. Each construction activity is tied to corresponding model components, as the timeline advances, elements appear, change state or highlight to reflect planned progress. Teams can scrub through the schedule visually and immediately grasp what happens when.

## Result

Site supervisors and project stakeholders can walk through the entire planned build sequence in a single review session. Clash detection and logistical conflicts that would have surfaced on-site are caught at the planning stage, reducing costly rework.
