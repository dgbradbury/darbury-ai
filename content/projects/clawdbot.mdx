## Problem

Generic AI chatbots give generic answers. Engineering clients asking questions about CAD standards, plant design codes, or AutoCAD workflows get responses calibrated for the general public — not for someone who's been in the industry for decades.

## Approach

ClawdBot is a domain-specialised chatbot framework built on Claude's API. It injects structured engineering knowledge into the system context — codes, standards, project-specific data — so the AI reasons within the right domain. OpenClaw is the open-source variant, designed for teams to deploy their own domain-tuned bots.

## Result

Responses that reference the correct standards, use the right terminology, and understand the constraints of the user's actual discipline — not a web-search summary of it.
