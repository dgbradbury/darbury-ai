## Problem

Standard CRM tools are built for sales teams, not engineering consultants. They don't understand project phases, technical deliverables, discipline expertise, or the long-cycle relationship patterns of consultancy work.

## Approach

Imhotep is a concept CRM where an AI layer understands engineering project lifecycles. It tracks not just contacts and deals but technical context: what standards a client works to, what their plant configuration is, what the last project's key lessons were. The AI surfaces relevant context before meetings and suggests follow-up based on project phase, not just last-contact date.

## Result

A system where the CRM becomes a technical memory — not just a contact database, but a project intelligence layer for the consultancy.
