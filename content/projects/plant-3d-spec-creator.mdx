## Problem

Creating and maintaining piping specifications in AutoCAD Plant 3D is labour-intensive. Spec writers must manually populate hundreds of component entries, cross-reference material standards, and keep data consistent as projects evolve — a process that takes weeks and introduces errors through repetition.

## Approach

Plant 3D Spec Creator automates the generation of complete piping specs from structured input data. It reads project-specific requirements — pressure classes, material grades, size ranges, applicable standards — and generates fully populated Plant 3D spec files with correct component data, end preparations, and bolting definitions.

## Result

Piping specs that previously required weeks of manual spec writing are generated in hours. The automated output is consistent, standards-compliant, and ready for immediate use in the Plant 3D project database.
