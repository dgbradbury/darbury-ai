## Problem

P&ID review is one of the most time-consuming tasks in plant engineering. Engineers manually check hundreds of symbols, tag numbers, and connections across complex diagrams — a process prone to fatigue-driven errors that can have serious downstream consequences.

## Approach

The P&ID Analyser processes piping and instrumentation diagrams through a multi-stage pipeline: image preprocessing to enhance symbol clarity, Claude AI for semantic understanding of diagram structure, and rule-based validation against project specifications. It identifies missing tags, inconsistent symbols, disconnected lines, and deviations from engineering standards automatically.

## Result

P&ID reviews that previously took an experienced engineer a full day now complete in under an hour, with AI flagging exceptions for human judgement rather than requiring exhaustive manual inspection. Error rates in issued documentation drop significantly.
